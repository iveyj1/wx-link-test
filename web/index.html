Waiting for receiver…

Rain: each tip is timed by the sensor and sent immediately; totals follow your browser's local midnight. If several tips arrive in one packet after radio loss, they are all credited at the time of the latest one.

“Instantaneous” means the latest completed full-revolution measurement, not an average of equal half-turns. Updates are radio-limited to about once per second. Idle heartbeat: 60 s. Gaps are not zero wind. Maintenance windows (shaded) are recorded but left out of graphs, averages and rain totals; manage them with the button above or maint.py. History retained for 7 days from server startup onward; only transmitted measurements are available. Times are estimated from reception time minus sensor-reported measurement age.
