The fit: slowdown per revolution against speed²

One dot per pair of neighbouring speed points. The model says these lie on a straight line: the intercept is 2a (friction) and the slope is 2b (drag).

The model

df/dt = −a − b·f²

f is the rotor speed in revolutions per second.

  • a (rev/s²) is a slowdown that is the same at every speed: dry friction, mainly from the bearing (shield or seal rub, rolling resistance of the balls).
  • b·f² grows with the square of speed, as air drag on the cups does, so b (1/rev) mainly describes the cups and arms.
  • The two balance at f = √(a/b), about 0.3 rev/s for the 608ZZ. Above that, drag dominates and removes a fixed fraction of the speed every revolution (about 9%), which gives the long tail; below it, bearing friction takes over and stops the rotor.

How it is fitted

Revolutions are counted exactly, so the fit uses revolution count n instead of time. Since df/dt = f·df/dn, the model becomes

d(f²)/dn = −2a − 2b·f²

which is a straight line in f². Each pair of consecutive speed points gives one value of Δ(f²)/Δn at the average f², and a weighted least-squares line through them gives a and b. No time derivatives are needed. Left out of the fit: revolutions while the hand is still on the rotor (everything up to one revolution after the last speed-up), the lopsided revolution that spans a stop, and speeds below 0.2 rev/s, where the magnet's pull on the reed switch and slowing within a single revolution distort things.

Solutions

Over time: f(t) = √(a/b) · tan( atan(f₀·√(b/a)) − √(ab)·t ). Over revolutions: f² = (f₀² + a/b)·e^(−2bn) − a/b, so a spin from f₀ stops after ln(1 + b·f₀²/a) / 2b revolutions.

Limits

  • a and b are rates of slowing down, not torques: converting needs the rotor's moment of inertia. The rotor is the same for every bearing, so comparing a compares friction directly.
  • A third term proportional to speed (grease drag) made the 608ZZ fit unstable and no better, so it is left out. A heavily greased bearing that the two-term model cannot fit would show as curvature on the fit chart.
  • b is for still air indoors; it is not a wind calibration.

Running a test

  1. Start a maintenance window on the dashboard, then bring the sensor indoors.
  2. Give 5 or more single pushes, each to at least 2 rev/s, letting it stop completely in between.
  3. Add the time range to spindown_sets.json (label, start, end, note); this page and spindown.py pick it up on reload.
