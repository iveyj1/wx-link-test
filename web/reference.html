System

Each packet is self-contained and cumulative (counts since boot), so a lost packet loses time resolution, never revolutions or tips.

Send sequence

  1. Wake: the first UART byte wakes the module from MODE=1 (the firmware never queries MODE while asleep).
  2. AT+SEND=2,<len>,<payload>, as one DMA transfer. The module splits a command at any idle gap of about one character, so byte-by-byte sends can break it.
  3. Wait up to 2.5 s for +OK, which means the frame has been transmitted.
  4. AT+MODE=1 puts it back to sleep.

Every setting is read back and checked after configuration. After 3 consecutive failures, and every 3rd one after that, NRST is pulsed and the module reconfigured.

Measured outdoors (sensor mounted, Nov 9–13): RSSI ≈ −93 dBm, SNR +5…+10 dB, packet loss 0.18%. Limits: payload ≤ 240 bytes (module); receiver line ≤ 255 characters.

Derived by the host: revolutions = (count − 1) ÷ 2 (the first closure is the reference), period = A + B, rotor frequency = 32768 ÷ period Hz. Worst-case length is 231 bytes (all u64 fields at 16 digits, caps applied).

The host rejects a frame on any inconsistency: wrong field count, period without revolutions, raw edges fewer than closures, out-of-range sensor values, pressure without temperature, or humidity without temperature.

Timing and logic

Timebase

  • RTC from the 32.768 kHz crystal (XOSC32K on GCLK2): 32768 ticks/s, extended to 64 bits. Every time in the packet uses it.
  • Crystal failover: if the RTC stays quiet for 8 s (it normally fires at least every 5 s), the RTC and the reed interrupt controller switch to the internal OSC32K (GCLK5) and clock becomes 1. The crystal is retried every 10 min. Condensation stopped it on Nov 3–5, before the board was conformal-coated.

Reed debounce (wind and rain)

  • Every edge interrupts, is counted in raw_edges, and restarts a 328-tick (10.01 ms) timer on the RTC compare.
  • A level change counts only if the pin holds it for the whole 10 ms. Bounce shorter than that is ignored.
  • Wind counts closures (falling edges); revolutions complete on closures 3, 5, 7…, with A and B the two half-periods. Two magnets or two closures per turn need not be evenly spaced, because A + B is always a full turn.
  • Rain counts one tip per debounced closure; the reopening isn't counted. A gauge resting closed at boot counts its next closure.
  • Limit: an open-circuit dropout of 10 ms or more inside a closure counts as an extra closure.

I2C bus: a software (bit-banged) master, because the SAMD21 Wire driver has no timeouts and hung the board with a faulty sensor (Nov 16). Every wait times out (20 ms of clock stretching), and each sensor access starts with a bus clear (9 clocks and a STOP). SDA and SCL are checked at boot; if either is low, I2C stays off and is re-checked every minute. Sensors are first probed at the first report, not in the first milliseconds after reset.

Counting carries on in interrupts during the delays. A reset that happens during any I2C access is taken as a stuck bus: both I2C sensors start off, report 0, and the bus is re-checked after 10 minutes. The SHT31 is re-probed after 3 failed reads in a row; its result is polled for up to ~110 ms (a genuine part needs 15.5 ms). The probe reads the status register first and sends Break and soft reset only if that fails.

After a software reset (a flash or !diag-reset) the sensor holds light sleep for 3 minutes. This host often does not notice the reset; opening and closing the port makes it re-enumerate.

Power modes and watchdog

  • Deep standby (battery): CPU, 48 MHz clock and SysTick stop; the RTC, reed interrupts and watchdog run and wake it.
  • Light sleep: used for the whole session if a USB host is seen within 10 s of boot. USB keeps working. To measure battery behaviour, unplug USB and then reset.
  • Watchdog: 16 s timeout from the ultra-low-power 32 kHz oscillator, with an early-warning interrupt at 8 s. The early warning also detects a dead crystal in deep standby. A stage trace in retained RAM survives a watchdog reset and is reported at the next boot.

Host processing

  • Wind: each new revolution becomes a sample (frequency, A/B imbalance). Calm is deduced from rev_age. Speed = frequency × calibration (set on the dashboard; currently 6.5 kn per rev/s).
  • Rain: tips added since the previous packet in the same session are stored with their time. A new session counts all its tips as new. Rain per tip: 0.369 mm (2 ml over a 49.5 × 109.5 mm funnel).
  • Pressure and temperature: one row per minute (BMP180 in the weather table, SHT31 in climate).
  • Gaps: no packet for 1 h is recorded as a data gap.
  • Maintenance windows (maint.py or the dashboard button) keep the data but hide it from graphs and totals. Sensor faults (fault.py; sensors wind, rain, baro, air) hide one sensor's readings only.

Rotor and calibration

  • 3 cups, radius ≈ 185 mm from the axis to cup centre; 2 reed closures per revolution.
  • Bearings: 608CE ceramic, dry, open, PTFE cage. A rough pair after reassembly; a new housing is planned.
  • Spin-down model: df/dt = −a − b·f². Here a is bearing friction (rev/s²) and b is cup drag (1/rev). The starting speed scales with √(a/b). Results are on the spin-down page.

Power

Measured on the earlier 1200 mAh LiPo near 4.1 V (about 1.2–1.8 mAh per mV, so treat currents as ±50%). The 400 mAh battery fitted on Nov 18 has about a third of the charge per mV, so expect the same currents to show about 3× these slopes and a charge to last about a third as long (roughly 1.5 weeks at the mixed rate).

USB commands (sensor and receiver)

One per line at 115200 baud. Wait 7 s or more after opening the port before sending !status.
